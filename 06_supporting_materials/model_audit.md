## Modeling conventions reviewed

- Blue font identifies editable analyst assumptions.
- Green font identifies cross-sheet formulas.
- Black font identifies same-sheet formulas and calculations.
- Historical data, assumptions, calculations, and outputs remain visibly separated.
- Sensitivity cells recalculate underlying transaction mechanics rather than using pasted values.
- Cross-sheet links use explicit worksheet references and no external workbook links.
- The workbook contains a source register for material public data inputs.

## Important limitations

- The transaction is hypothetical and has not been proposed by either company.
- Rockwell's buyer forecast uses a limited set of publicly disclosed metrics rather than a full three-statement forecast.
- Purchase-price allocation is illustrative and would require third-party valuation work at closing.
- Synergies are analyst assumptions based on identifiable cost pools and commercial logic, not management guidance.
- Financing terms, tax treatment, fees, and close timing could differ in an actual transaction.
- Market prices and company forecasts are based on the analysis date stated in the workbook and will become stale.

These limitations are disclosed so the model demonstrates analytical judgment without implying false precision.
