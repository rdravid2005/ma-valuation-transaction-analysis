# Trading Comparable Companies Analysis

## Purpose

The trading comparable companies analysis estimates Cognex's value by comparing it with public companies that have similar products, customers, end markets, business models, or financial characteristics. Unlike a DCF, which derives value from forecast cash flow and a discount rate, trading comps reflect how the market currently prices comparable businesses.

## Peer-selection framework

No public company is a perfect Cognex comparable. The selected set therefore combines direct strategic relevance with broader industrial-technology comparability.

| Company | Classification | Why it is included | Principal limitation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Keyence | Closest strategic / machine vision | Direct machine-vision, sensing, and factory-automation exposure | Much higher margin and premium valuation profile |
| Teledyne Technologies | Imaging / instrumentation | High-quality imaging, sensing, and instrumentation portfolio | Larger and more diversified |
| Zebra Technologies | Machine vision / data capture | Machine vision, barcode identification, and data-capture overlap | Different end-market and leverage profile |
| AMETEK | Precision instruments / automation | Premium industrial instrumentation and automation reference | Acquisition-driven diversified portfolio |
| Fortive | Industrial technology / instrumentation | Relevant industrial-technology and instrumentation exposure | Broader portfolio and different growth mix |
| Trimble | Industrial software / sensing | Technology-enabled industrial workflows and sensing | Greater software mix; LTM P/E is not meaningful |
| Rockwell Automation | Industrial automation platform | Relevant automation benchmark and hypothetical buyer | Much larger platform; not an independent target peer |

Keyence is analytically important but should not determine the offer price by itself. Its 52.1% LTM EBIT margin and 14.5x EV/Revenue multiple are well above the group medians, demonstrating why bankers examine quartiles and company-specific differences rather than applying the highest multiple.

## Selected trading statistics

| Metric | 25th percentile | Median | 75th percentile |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| EV / Revenue | 4.5x | 5.2x | 6.9x |
| EV / EBITDA | 17.7x | 19.7x | 23.9x |
| EV / EBIT | 24.1x | 27.5x | 28.9x |
| P / E | 33.4x | 36.3x | 39.3x |

The analysis uses current/LTM public-company multiples and applies them to Cognex's FY2026E metrics. This is a practical public-data approximation; in a live banking assignment, the preferred presentation would use calendarized consensus estimates from a market-data platform so peer and target periods match exactly.

## Implied Cognex valuation

| Method | Low | Median | High |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| EV / Revenue | $34.75 | $39.79 | $50.94 |
| EV / EBITDA | $39.21 | $43.14 | $51.37 |
| EV / EBIT | $46.95 | $52.91 | $55.33 |
| P / E | $49.89 | $54.28 | $58.74 |

The core trading-comps indication is the median EV/Revenue to median EV/EBITDA range of approximately **$39.79 to $43.14 per share**. Those enterprise-value methods are emphasized because they reduce distortions from differences in capital structure, interest expense, and tax profiles. EV/EBIT and P/E provide corroborative evidence but are not the primary offer-price anchors.

## Interpretation

The core comps range is approximately 54%–67% above the standalone DCF value of $25.79 per share, with a midpoint premium of approximately 61%. That difference is not automatically evidence that one method is wrong:

- The DCF is highly sensitive to the 11.375% WACC and long-term assumptions.
- Trading multiples incorporate current market sentiment and peer growth expectations.
- Cognex's FY2026E forecast includes a substantial earnings recovery, which raises forward EBITDA and EPS.
- The peer set contains premium businesses whose quality and diversification may exceed Cognex's.

The next valuation steps will test this gap using precedent transactions and a valuation summary. No acquisition offer should be selected from trading comps alone.

## Audit notes

- Market data are dated August 2026 and sourced through entries S-032 to S-038.
- Equity and enterprise values are presented in each company's local currency; valuation multiples are currency-neutral.
- Keyence is excluded from the EV/EBITDA quartiles because a current LTM EBITDA multiple was unavailable in the selected public source.
- All implied share prices use formula-linked Cognex forecast metrics, cash, debt, and diluted shares from the model.
